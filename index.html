Lahori Gate · Shahjahanabad
The monument

Built for an emperor. Remembered by a nation.

Known in Hindi as Lal Qila, the Red Fort was commissioned by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan when he moved his capital to Shahjahanabad. Its red sandstone walls still frame a remarkable collection of palaces, audience halls and gardens.

For generations, it was the seat of Mughal power. In 1947, it became a powerful symbol of a newly independent India—a place where the Prime Minister addresses the nation each Independence Day.

Then & now

History that still gathers people.

The Red Fort is not only a monument to the Mughal period. It is also a place where India's past and present meet. Its annual Independence Day ceremony connects a historic landmark with a living national tradition.
